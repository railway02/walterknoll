Our purpose

Furniture is part of everyday life. The collection includes places to sit, work and spend time with other people.

Wood, leather and fabric give each piece a different look and feel.

See the materials

History

  • 1865: Wilhelm Knoll opens a leather business in Stuttgart.
  • 1925: Walter Knoll establishes his own furniture company.
  • 1949: The upholstered Vostra joins the postwar collection.
  • 1985: Walter Knoll takes over Wilhelm Knoll.

Source: Walter Knoll history.
